A serverless image compression service

Setup Instructions

  1. Open the Bandwidth Guardian settings in your browser.
  2. In the Proxy URL input, enter:
    https://your-netlify-domain.netlify.app/api/index
  3. Save settings and verify compression in your extension menu.
